隐私政策 · Privacy Policy

Lang Study（lang分析）· 生效日期 Effective 2026-11-02

要点 / In short. 我们只收集运行账号和学习同步所必需的数据：你的邮箱、登录会话、你自己保存的学习记录。没有广告，没有第三方分析或追踪，不出售数据。

We collect only what an account and cross-device study sync need: your email address, sign-in sessions, and the study records you create. No ads, no third-party analytics or trackers, and we do not sell data.

1. 谁在运营 · Who runs this

Lang Study（中文名 lang分析）由个人开发者 Asher Huang 运营，站点为 lang.asher.place。隐私相关问题请联系 huangyea@gmail.com。

Lang Study is operated by an individual developer, Asher Huang, at lang.asher.place. Questions about privacy: huangyea@gmail.com.

2. 我们收集什么 · What we collect

账号与登录 · Account and sign-in

  • 邮箱地址（用于一次性登录验证码）、可选显示名、邮箱验证状态、账号创建与更新时间。
  • 登录验证码（6 位数字，约 5 分钟过期，最多尝试 3 次）。
  • 会话记录：会话标识、创建与过期时间（30 天）、IP 地址、浏览器或设备 User-Agent，用于「设备列表」和「退出其他设备」。
  • 可选通行密钥（passkey）：公钥凭据标识。指纹或面容数据始终留在你的设备上，我们从不接收。

Email address (used for one-time sign-in codes), optional display name, verification status, and account timestamps. One-time 6-digit codes that expire in about five minutes. Session records: identifier, creation and expiry (30 days), IP address and browser or device user-agent, used for the device list and remote sign-out. Optional passkeys store only a public credential; biometric data never leaves your device.

你的学习数据 · Study data you create

  • 收藏的句子与词汇、复习安排（间隔重复的评级与到期时间）、阅读进度与界面偏好中你选择同步的部分。
  • 同步所需的记录版本号与操作回执，用于在你多台设备之间可靠地合并改动。
  • 课程正文、字幕与音视频不随账号上传；这些内容由站点直接提供，保存的学习记录只引用页面中的位置。

Saved sentences and vocabulary, review scheduling (spaced-repetition grades and due dates), reading position, and any synced preferences. Record revisions and operation receipts needed to merge changes reliably across your devices. Lesson text, subtitles, audio and video are served by the site and are not uploaded with your account; saved records only reference positions in a page.

安全与滥用防护 · Security and abuse prevention

  • 登录与设备换取接口的短时计数器（按 IP 地址），以及每日验证码发送数量计数。
  • 原生 App 登录交接使用的一次性代码，仅以哈希形式保存，短期过期后自动删除。

Short-lived counters keyed by IP address for the sign-in and device-exchange endpoints, plus a daily count of codes sent. One-time hand-off codes for native app sign-in are stored only as hashes and are purged after they expire.

我们不收集 · What we do not collect

  • 不收集广告标识符，不做跨站追踪，不接入第三方分析 SDK。
  • 不请求照片、相机、麦克风、通讯录、精确位置或健康数据。
  • 不读取你设备上的其他 App 或文件。

No advertising identifiers, no cross-site tracking, no third-party analytics SDKs. We do not request photos, camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, or health data, and we do not read other apps or files on your device.

3. 数据保存在哪里 · Where the data lives

账号与同步数据存放在 Cloudflare（Workers 与 D1 数据库）位于美国的区域服务中。登录验证码通过邮件服务商发送。除运行服务所必需的处理者外，我们不会把数据提供给第三方，也不会出售或用于广告。

旧版课程页面提供可选的「收藏备份到 GitHub Gist」：只有你主动连接并授权时才会写入，数据保存在你自己的 GitHub 账号中，受 GitHub 的隐私政策约束。

Account and sync data are stored in Cloudflare Workers and D1. Sign-in codes are delivered through an email provider. Beyond the processors needed to run the service, we do not share data with third parties, sell it, or use it for advertising. The legacy lesson pages offer an optional favorites backup to a GitHub gist: it is written only if you connect it, lives in your own GitHub account, and is governed by GitHub's privacy policy.

4. 保留期限 · Retention

  • 登录会话：30 天后过期；你也可以随时在设备列表中退出其他设备。
  • 学习记录：保留到你删除该条记录或删除账号为止。
  • 过期的一次性登录代码与过期的限流窗口：每天自动清理。
  • 删除账号时，账号、会话与学习记录一并删除。

Sessions expire after 30 days, and you can sign out other devices at any time from the device list. Study records are kept until you delete them or delete your account. Expired one-time codes and stale rate-limit windows are purged daily. Deleting your account deletes the account, its sessions, and its study records.

5. 你的选择 · Your choices

  • 删除单条收藏或学习记录，或在设置中清除本机数据。
  • 退出其他设备上的登录。
  • 删除整个账号：目前请发邮件到 huangyea@gmail.com，我们会在 30 天内删除。App 内自助删除会在后续版本提供。

Delete individual saved items or clear local data in settings. Sign out other devices. To delete your whole account, email huangyea@gmail.com and we will delete it within 30 days; in-app deletion is planned for a later update.

6. 儿童 · Children

本服务面向语言学习者，不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集其个人信息。如果你认为有儿童向我们提供了信息，请联系我们删除。

The service is intended for language learners and is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. 安全 · Security

传输使用 HTTPS。登录凭据与服务密钥不会出现在页面代码中；服务端会话令牌经过签名，设备交接代码一次性且短时有效。没有任何系统能保证绝对安全，请为你的账号使用你能控制的邮箱。

Traffic uses HTTPS. Credentials and service secrets are not shipped in page code; server session tokens are signed, and device hand-off codes are single-use and short-lived. No system is perfectly secure, so use an email address you control.

8. 变更 · Changes

本政策如有实质变更，会更新本页顶部的生效日期，并在站点上说明。继续使用服务即表示接受更新后的政策。

If this policy changes materially, the effective date at the top of this page is updated and the change is noted on the site. Continuing to use the service means accepting the updated policy.
